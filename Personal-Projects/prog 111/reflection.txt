Please respond thoroughly (at least two sentences for each question) to
the following three questions.

1. Describe an experience you had this semester where you saw the power of
functions.
In the water flow assignments where we calculated psi of water flow using functions was probably the first time I realized how useful and powerful they are. In this final project as well I did see that as well as the power of integrating them in GUI.


2. In your judgment, what is the value of test functions?
It is pretty valuable for hard-coded programs or programs that receive no user input. Outside of that, test functions are really hard to use. In those cases, I would prefer troubleshooting manually.



3. Describe your process for troubleshooting and fixing a program that
has a problem. What is one thing you can do to improve your process?
First, I try to see whats wrong in the code myself. Second, if I have test functions set up, I try using them as well as except statements. After that, I ask co-pilot to tell me what I am doing wrong.
